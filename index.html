Privacy Policy — Deadeye Duel

Effective date: November 11, 2026
Developer: Avery Robinson (Ripthegame)
Contact: sirmastercuts@gmail.com

Deadeye Duel is a Western quick-draw dueling game. This policy explains what the app does — and does not do — with your information. It is short because the app collects almost nothing.

What the app collects

Nothing. Deadeye Duel does not collect, store, share, or transmit any personal data. Specifically:

  • No accounts. You never sign in, and the app never asks for your name, email, or any other personal information.
  • No analytics or tracking. The app contains no analytics, advertising, or tracking software of any kind.
  • No location, camera, microphone, or contacts. The app does not request any device permissions.
  • No game data leaves your device. Coins, XP, levels, and progress exist only while you are playing. The app does not save any data to your device and does not send any data anywhere — it works fully offline.

Network use

The app makes no network requests of its own and works without an internet connection. The game includes a credits page with links to outside websites (for example, the sound-effect sources and reference games). Those links open in your device's web browser, outside the app, and those websites have their own privacy policies. Tapping a link is the only way the app ever causes a network connection.

Children

Deadeye Duel is a game about Western gun duels and is not directed at children under 13. Because the app collects no information from anyone, it collects no information from children either.

In-app purchases

The current version of Deadeye Duel has no real-money purchases. The in-game shop, Armory, and game passes all run on coins earned by playing the game. If paid purchases are added in a future version, this policy will be updated before they go live.

Data deletion

There is nothing to delete: the app stores no personal data and no account exists. Uninstalling the app removes the game from your device.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date. Continued use of the app after a change means you accept the new policy.
